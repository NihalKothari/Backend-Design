# Capacity Planning and Forecasting

## Why it matters
Capacity planning prevents outages during growth and spikes.

## Progression checkpoints
| Level | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Beginner | Monitor resource usage. |
| Intermediate | Forecast growth trends. |
| Senior | Plan capacity with headroom. |
| Principal | Define capacity review cadence. |

## Key concepts
- Baseline usage and peak traffic.
- Growth forecasting.
- Headroom and safety margins.

## Real-world example: Holiday traffic
A retailer forecasts 2x traffic and increases capacity before the season.

## Practical checklist
- Keep 20-30% headroom for spikes.
- Review capacity monthly.
- Track scaling costs and limits.
