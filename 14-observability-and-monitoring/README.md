# Observability and Monitoring

This module focuses on measuring system health and troubleshooting issues.

Topics covered:
- Metrics, logs, and traces and how they work together.
- Instrumentation and structured logging.
- Distributed tracing and correlation IDs.
- SLIs, SLOs, and alerting strategies.
- Dashboards and operational KPIs.
- Signal-to-noise practices for alerts.
- Monitoring tools, exporters, and agents.
- Log retention, sampling, and privacy considerations.
