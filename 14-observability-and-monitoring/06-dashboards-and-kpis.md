# Dashboards and KPIs

## Why it matters
Dashboards provide a shared view of system health and business impact.

## Progression checkpoints
| Level | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Beginner | Build simple dashboards. |
| Intermediate | Add KPIs and service health. |
| Senior | Create role-specific dashboards. |
| Principal | Define standard dashboards. |

## Key concepts
- Service health dashboards.
- Business KPIs and technical metrics.
- Drill-down and filtering.

## Real-world example: Checkout dashboard
A dashboard shows success rate, latency, and payment provider errors.

## Practical checklist
- Keep dashboards focused and readable.
- Link dashboards to on-call runbooks.
- Review KPIs with stakeholders regularly.
